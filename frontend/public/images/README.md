# Creator Photos Guide

## 📁 Place Your Photos Here

Put your 4 creator photos in this folder with these exact names:

- `creator-photo1.jpg` - Innovation & Vision
- `creator-photo2.jpg` - Technical Excellence  
- `creator-photo3.jpg` - Research & Development
- `creator-photo4.jpg` - Future Vision

## 📐 Recommended Photo Specs

- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Size**: 400x400px minimum (square format works best)
- **File Size**: Under 1MB each for fast loading
- **Quality**: High resolution for crisp display

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **With Photos**: When you add photos with the correct names, they'll automatically appear on the About page
2. **Without Photos**: If photos are missing, it shows stylish letter placeholders (K, A, R, A)
3. **Fallback**: If a photo fails to load, it gracefully falls back to the letter placeholder

## 🎨 Photo Tips

- Use professional headshots or high-quality photos
- Ensure good lighting and clear facial features
- Square crop works best for the circular display
- Consider consistent styling across all 4 photos

## 🔗 Current Photo Paths

The About page looks for photos at:
- `/images/creator-photo1.jpg`
- `/images/creator-photo2.jpg` 
- `/images/creator-photo3.jpg`
- `/images/creator-photo4.jpg`

Just drop your photos here and refresh the page!
